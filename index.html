good to know

Local island life.

👙Swimwear at the tourist beach

Himmafushi is a local island. Bikinis are fine at the designated tourist beach; cover shoulders and knees around the village.

🥤Alcohol-free island

Like all inhabited islands in the Maldives, there's no alcohol here. Fresh juices and iced coffee instead.

🕌Prayer times

Shops close briefly during prayer times. Friday mornings are quiet.

🧾Taxes included in your total

Your quote shows tourism GST and the per-night green tax up front, so there are no surprises at checkout.
